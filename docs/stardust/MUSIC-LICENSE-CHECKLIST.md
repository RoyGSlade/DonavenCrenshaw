# Suno music rights and evidence checklist

**Checked:** 2026-09-26. This is a production record checklist, not legal advice. Suno changed its download policy and Terms effective September 3, 2026, so older help text can use a different test. Before a release, re-check the linked official sources and retain a dated copy of the terms that apply to the track.

## The practical rule for this project

For a soundtrack asset we plan to ship commercially, use a Suno **paid plan at creation and at the approved download**, and preserve evidence of both. Current official wording ties commercial use to songs downloaded while subscribed/on a paid plan; Suno’s ownership help also says Pro/Premier subscribers are considered owners of songs created while subscribed. The September 2026 Terms say commercial exploitation requires a permitted download through an approved channel and that qualifying commercial-use rights are perpetual. Keeping both dates satisfied avoids betting a release on the differences in the official FAQ wording.

Do not assume that paying later retroactively licenses an earlier free-plan generation. Suno’s retroactivity FAQ says a later Pro/Premier subscription does not grant retroactive commercial-use rights by default; any exception is specific and not guaranteed. If a track was generated free, do not ship it commercially unless Suno gives track-specific written permission that has been reviewed and saved.

Commercial-use permission is different from copyright protection. Suno says paid subscribers are considered owners/receive its rights in qualifying Output, but its Terms make no warranty that copyright vests in the Output. Its copyright FAQ says fully AI-generated music may not qualify for U.S. copyright protection because the human authorship requirement applies. A paid plan is not a promise of exclusive copyright against third parties, a guarantee against similarity claims, or a substitute for checking the game’s release platform rules.

## Per-track release checklist

- [ ] Identify the exact account, plan tier, and paid subscription interval. Save the invoice/receipt and account evidence.
- [ ] Record the generation date/time, Suno song URL/ID, title, model/options if shown, and the prompt/Exclude text. Confirm paid status at creation.
- [ ] Download the selected output through Suno’s approved download control while subscribed to a paid plan. Record date/time, format, and any download allocation used. Keep the downloaded original unchanged.
- [ ] Confirm the output is not a Remix of another user’s song and does not include uploaded material, a voice model, lyrics, samples, or other inputs unless their rights and the additional terms have been cleared. Current Suno terms say Remixes have separate non-commercial limits.
- [ ] Save the applicable Terms of Service and official rights-help pages as dated PDFs/screenshots or exports; retain the URLs and access date in the track record.
- [ ] Keep the prompt and final edit notes, original download, accepted WAV master, any edited/exported copy, and hashes together. Do not remove or obscure Suno provenance metadata/fingerprints from the source file; if editing for the game, retain that unmodified source and document derivative edits.
- [ ] Review the output for recognizable third-party melodies, lyrics, samples, voices, or unwanted similarities. Reject or seek legal review if anything appears to use protected material.
- [ ] Re-check the target storefront/distributor’s current AI-content rules and rights warranties before release.
- [ ] Track each shipped file to its Suno ID, proof folder, edit version, and game path. Do not bundle an unqualified free-plan generation into a commercial build.

## Claim distinctions

| Claim | What the official material supports | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial use | Suno says qualifying paid-plan downloads may be monetized, including in video games; commercial rights do not require an ongoing subscription after they attach to a qualifying track/download. | That every download, tier, remix, or output is commercially cleared; that another platform must accept it. |
| Creation date / subscription | Ownership help describes songs created while subscribed to Pro/Premier as owned by the subscriber. Current download guidance also conditions commercial rights on paid-plan downloads. Preserve both creation and download evidence. | That a subscription started later automatically changes the status of earlier free generations. |
| Ownership | Under current Terms, Suno assigns its interest in qualifying Output from paid-tier submissions, subject to the Terms. | A blanket promise of copyrightability, exclusivity from other users, or third-party non-infringement. |
| Copyright | Suno explicitly distinguishes its commercial permission/assignment from whether copyright exists; it makes no copyright warranty. | A definitive legal conclusion for every jurisdiction or human contribution. |

## Official sources checked 2026-09-26

- [What rights do I have with a paid subscription?](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9601665) — current help wording: songs downloaded while subscribed receive commercial-use rights; includes games. Edited 2026-09-03; accessed 2026-09-26.
- [Does Suno own the music I make?](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2416769) — Pro/Premier creation-while-subscribed ownership description; says rights persist after subscription ends. Edited 2026-01-07; accessed 2026-09-26.
- [If I subscribe, do I get rights for songs I made before subscribing?](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2425729) — no retroactive rights by default; exceptions are not guaranteed. Edited 2025-12-17; accessed 2026-09-26.
- [Do I have the copyrights to songs I made?](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2746945) — ownership vs copyright distinction and copyright uncertainty. Edited 2026-09-04; accessed 2026-09-26.
- [Suno: an update to our downloads policy and Terms of Service](https://about.suno.com/blog/suno-updates-tos) — September 3, 2026 download-policy change, paid downloads and trial-download limits. Published August 2026; accessed 2026-09-26.

Where Suno’s help pages use older “created while subscribed” language while the current paid-rights help and September 2026 Terms discuss a paid-plan download, keep the stronger evidence set: paid at creation **and** at download. Do not infer retroactive coverage from either subscription status today or a later billing receipt.
