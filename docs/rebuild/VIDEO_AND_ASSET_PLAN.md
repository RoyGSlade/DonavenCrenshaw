# Video and visual asset plan

No placeholder media is rendered. The plan below is a proof backlog; an item becomes public only when the real file, caption, alt text, and transcript exist.

## Priority capture plan

| Priority | Asset | Proof it must show | Required accessible companion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | BetterFingers 60–90 second workflow | Clean launch, dictation, transcription, refinement, review, injection, result | Edited transcript, captions, text summary, limitations |
| 2 | BetterFingers short installation clip | Installer source, install, first launch, permissions, uninstall/recovery | Transcript and exact tested version/platform |
| 3 | BetterFingers screenshots | Overlay, review window, active/inactive tray states | Descriptive alt text and screenshot captions |
| 4 | Founder introduction | Donaven, mission, three branches, present focus | Full transcript and poster image |
| 5 | Crenshaw Systems workflow demonstration | Diagnosis intake to practical deliverable using non-client or authorized data | Transcript, sample files, confidentiality clearance |
| 6 | Infinite Ages product images | Representative pages from the real PDFs | Captions and license confirmation |
| 7 | GetFast/PDFManager demos | Real implemented path only | Tested build/platform, transcript, limitations |

## Production rules

- Record the full workflow, including failure/recovery, not a montage that hides gaps.
- State the exact build/version/date and tested platform in the caption.
- Never use private client, employer, healthcare, or credential-bearing material.
- Supply captions and a human-reviewed transcript. Do not depend on auto-captions alone.
- Use a static poster and click-to-play embed; no autoplay.
- Host locally or use a privacy-conscious external player only after its network/privacy behavior is documented.
- Compress images to modern formats when appropriate while preserving original proof files outside the site artifact.

## Existing asset disposition

- Keep the BetterFingers app and tray PNGs; use them only with current product context.
- Keep and link the two Infinite Ages PDFs. Do not claim a license that is not attached to them.
- Keep the sprite/audio tree out of the primary experience until the associated demo is intentionally tested and archived.
- Do not use the old missing image references or placeholder YouTube record from legacy media JSON.
- `.playwright-cli/` is ephemeral session state. Reviewed QA captures belong in `output/playwright/`.
