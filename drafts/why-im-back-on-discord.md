Back in February I said I was stepping away from Discord over its age-verification push and the third-party breach. I meant it. Now I'm walking that statement back, and I'd rather say so out loud than quietly pretend I never wrote it.

Here's the short version. As far as I can tell, Discord walked back the age-verification plan that got me out the door. That was the line for me, so I'm back. (Source link goes here once I've dug it up. Don't take my word for it, go read it.)

The practical reason is boring: that's where people already are. Stardust players, playtesters, friends. Building a whole separate comms platform so a handful of people can trade lap times was never going to win, and I'd rather ship games than stall on a chat app.

So the server is half-built. Channels are a mess, the rules are short, and I'm still figuring out the layout. Come anyway. Rules are on the support page and the summary is: be decent, keep it SFW, no spam.

And I'm watching. If Discord tries the age-verification thing again, or anything like it, I'm gone again. No speech, no drama, just a new invite link somewhere else.

Same policy as before: I don't trust a platform, I trust what it does. So far it's doing fine. Ask me again next quarter.
