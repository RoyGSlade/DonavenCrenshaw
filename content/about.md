        <p class="section-label">FOUNDER</p>
        <h2>Donaven Crenshaw</h2>
        <p>I make games and useful free software. Right now that means Stardust, a browser racer you can play with your friends, Kingdoms &amp; Caravans, a city-builder in early playtest, and BetterFingers, a local-first dictation app that started as an accessibility tool.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="noir-card noir-card--lit about-mission">
      <p class="section-label">THE HONEST VERSION</p>
      <div class="reading-text">
        <p>My work comes in bursts. I get an idea and build hard for a few weeks, then life gets in the way, or I get overexcited about the next idea, or something breaks and I get fed up and walk away for a while. My project history has gaps you could fly a ship through.</p>
        <p>But I always come back. Building things that make people happy is the part of me that doesn't switch off, and every return starts a little further along than the last one.</p>
        <p>So this site shows the real state of things: what works, what doesn't yet, and what I'm working on next, instead of polished promises.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
  </section>

  <section class="section-block about-story" aria-labelledby="story-so-far">
    <div class="section-header">
      <div>
        <p class="section-label">FROM THE COMMIT HISTORY</p>
        <h2 id="story-so-far" class="section-title">The story so far</h2>
        <p class="section-desc">Told from the repositories, gaps included.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="reading-text">
      <ul>
        <li><strong>2022–2023:</strong> first games. A space game project, a pile of minigames, and a top-down 2D game in Godot.</li>
        <li><strong>2023–2024:</strong> Infinite Ages, my tabletop RPG, and a text-based single-player version of it. Then almost a year of nothing.</li>
        <li><strong>2025:</strong> back with AI-driven game experiments, a first RTS attempt, and a portfolio site that turned into a small game platform: a starmap flying game, riddles, a hall of fame and a text RPG. That starmap game is where Stardust began.</li>
        <li><strong>Mid 2026:</strong> BetterFingers got serious: a rebuild, a long security and reliability hardening sprint, and a signed public alpha. Infinite Ages Evolved got a web portal and game-master tools.</li>
        <li><strong>September 2026:</strong> Kingdoms &amp; Caravans reached a friend playtest. Its infrastructure turned out to be the hard part, so Stardust took the lead: five circuits, accounts, leaderboards, Dogfight rooms, and now friends and challenges, to get more people playing and coming back.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>
