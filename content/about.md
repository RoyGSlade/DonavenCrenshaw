<p class="section-desc">Software builder, systems thinker, and maker of practical technology.</p>

<div class="reading-text">
<p>Donaven Crenshaw is a software builder based in Northern Nevada. His work connects local AI, workflow improvement, accessibility, games, and creative technology.</p>
<p>Experience in healthcare operations informs an interest in clear handoffs, humane workflows, and tools that reduce friction without exposing sensitive information. Any healthcare-oriented work here is presented only as a sanitized workflow prototype.</p>
<p>BetterFingers originated in a desire to help a family member communicate more easily while gaming and using online applications. That practical need continues to shape the project: the person using the software should stay in control of what it hears, writes, and sends.</p>
