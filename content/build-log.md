      <div class="reading-text buildlog-entry-copy">
        <p>The priority moved to Stardust and this site: get more people to find the games, play them, and come back with friends.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>The homepage now leads with Stardust and a Play button, with the current times to beat.</li>
          <li>The game tells you plainly when a time wasn't saved and why, instead of failing quietly.</li>
          <li>Dogfight guests got smoother movement. Their own ship responding immediately, instead of waiting on the host, arrives with the next relay update.</li>
          <li>Rolling out: friends, challenge links you can send back and forth, medals (17, 19 and 21 seconds for gold, silver and bronze on each circuit), a handful of silly achievements, and a playtest lab for trying new boost, fuel and rail rules before any of them become real.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Next: a friend playtest of all of that, a better after-match lobby for Dogfight, and a custom track.</p>
