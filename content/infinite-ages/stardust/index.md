<section class="sd-section" id="how-it-plays" aria-labelledby="sd-how-title">
  <div class="sd-section-head"><h2 id="sd-how-title">How it plays</h2></div>
  <p class="sd-copy">Each field starts on a launch pad. Launch, pick up all five shards, and fly into the gate to move on. Thrust burns fuel, and running dry costs you thirty seconds and restarts the field, so the fuel stations matter. Boost gives a short push and recharges on its own.</p>
