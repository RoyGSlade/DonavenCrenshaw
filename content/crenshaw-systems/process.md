<p class="section-desc">Start with the system problem. A software request is not the same thing as a useful diagnosis.</p>

<section class="section-block" aria-labelledby="intake">
  <div class="section-header"><div><h2 id="intake" class="section-title">Exact intake</h2><p class="section-desc">Use the worksheet when it is supplied, or include this context directly in your email.</p></div></div>
  <div class="reading-text">
    <p>Email <a href="mailto:dcworks@donavencrenshaw.com">dcworks@donavencrenshaw.com</a> and include, as clearly as you can:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>what is going wrong;</li>
      <li>what task or process consumes too much time;</li>
      <li>who performs it;</li>
      <li>how often it happens;</li>
      <li>the tools currently involved;</li>
      <li>what you have already tried;</li>
      <li>the consequences of leaving it unfixed;</li>
      <li>the desired outcome;</li>
      <li>timing and approximate budget, if known;</li>
      <li>whether I may share the journey publicly;</li>
      <li>confidentiality requirements;</li>
      <li>integration or training needs.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>I check fit from the message. An email alone does not promise a solution.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section-block" aria-labelledby="discovery">
  <div class="section-header"><div><h2 id="discovery" class="section-title">Initial discovery</h2><p class="section-desc">$100 · up to two hours · separate paid service</p></div><span class="section-subtitle mono">DIAGNOSIS FIRST</span></div>
  <div class="reading-text"><p>Approved leads receive a discovery invitation. The session can take up to two hours so I can understand the workflow, constraints, risks, and actual root cause. There is no guaranteed solution before that understanding exists.</p><p>After discovery, any deeper assessment, prototype, implementation, integration, documentation, training, licensing, and aftercare are handled through a scoped proposal. I do not publish invented terms or provisional prices for those stages.</p></div>
</section>

<section class="section-block" aria-labelledby="service-sequence">
  <div class="section-header"><div><h2 id="service-sequence" class="section-title">The sequence</h2><p class="section-desc">Each stage follows from what the diagnosis actually shows.</p></div></div>
  <ol class="reading-text">
    <li>Systems diagnosis</li>
    <li>Root-cause analysis</li>
    <li>Solution design</li>
    <li>Implementation planning</li>
    <li>Software development when appropriate</li>
    <li>Integration</li>
    <li>Documentation</li>
    <li>Training</li>
    <li>Aftercare</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="section-block" aria-labelledby="ownership">
  <div class="section-header"><div><h2 id="ownership" class="section-title">Commercial boundaries</h2><p class="section-desc">The public page states the order; final documents state the legal terms.</p></div></div>
  <div class="reading-text"><p>Crenshaw Systems does not accept solutions dependent on ongoing third-party subscriptions. A recommendation may be an existing non-subscription tool, a scoped implementation, or no build at all.</p><p>Formal contracts, invoices, proposals, licenses, confidentiality terms, payment structures, warranty, and aftercare boundaries use the formal name <em>Donaven Crenshaw Systems Consulting</em>. Those details are not invented on this page.</p></div>
</section>
