    <div class="reading-text">
    <p>Email <a href="mailto:dcworks@donavencrenshaw.com">dcworks@donavencrenshaw.com</a> and include, as clearly as you can:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>what is going wrong;</li>
      <li>what task or process consumes too much time;</li>
      <li>who performs it;</li>
      <li>how often it happens;</li>
      <li>the tools currently involved;</li>
      <li>what you have already tried;</li>
      <li>the consequences of leaving it unfixed;</li>
      <li>the desired outcome;</li>
      <li>timing and approximate budget, if known;</li>
      <li>whether I may share the journey publicly;</li>
      <li>confidentiality requirements;</li>
      <li>integration or training needs.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>I check fit from the message. An email alone does not promise a solution.</p>

  <div class="section-header"><div><h2 id="discovery" class="section-title">Initial discovery</h2><p class="section-desc">$100 · up to two hours · separate paid service</p></div><span class="section-subtitle mono">DIAGNOSIS FIRST</span></div>
  <div class="noir-card reading-text crenshaw-copy-card"><p>Approved leads receive a discovery invitation. The session can take up to two hours so I can understand the workflow, constraints, risks, and actual root cause. There is no guaranteed solution before that understanding exists.</p><p>After discovery, any deeper assessment, prototype, implementation, integration, documentation, training, licensing, and aftercare are handled through a scoped proposal. I do not publish invented terms or provisional prices for those stages.</p></div>
  </section>
