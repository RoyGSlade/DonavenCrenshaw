<p class="section-desc">The independent software lab of Donaven Crenshaw.</p>
<div class="reading-text">
<p>Source Arcanum is a home for local-first experiments, practical applications, games, and build notes. It is a lab name, not a substitute for the person making the work.</p>
<p>The guiding idea is straightforward: people should be able to understand the limits of their tools, keep control of their data, and continue using useful software without unnecessary accounts, tracking, or permanent subscriptions.</p>
<p>That does not mean every project is finished or fully offline today. Each project directory identifies its maturity and links so visitors can decide whether it is ready for their needs.</p>
</div>
