## Primary recurring support

[Support on GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/RoyGSlade) is the primary recurring support channel.

Support helps fund free-software polishing, releases, compute, hosting, signing, testing, server infrastructure, and other project needs. Funding is project-based and does not guarantee a date or a particular release.

## Support levels

- **$5 Supporter** — a simple recurring way to keep the work going.
- **$15 Builder** — helps cover the steady work of testing, documentation, and releases.
- **$50 Sustainer** — helps provide more room for focused development and infrastructure.

These names describe support amounts, not ownership or control. There is no voting mechanic on this page. A contribution does not buy roadmap authority, a guaranteed feature, a deadline, or a private product edition.

## One-time support and future merch

[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/democratizegm) is available for one-time support. It may also be used for future merch if and when real products exist; no merch inventory, reward, shipping schedule, or eligibility is promised here.

The site does not advertise a private Discord benefit because access and moderation rules are not published. Support should remain useful even when no extra perk exists.
