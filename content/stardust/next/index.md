<section class="sn-skills" aria-labelledby="sn-skills-title">
  <div class="pf-section-head"><p class="pf-eyebrow">Eight skills · fifteen hands-on games</p><h2 id="sn-skills-title">You do the work, not a dice roll.</h2></div>
  <p>Every skill is something you play. The lines below are the game's own.</p>
  <ul class="sn-skill-list">
    <li style="--c:#FF7A59"><strong>Shooting</strong><span>Your hands are the skill; the points tune the gun.</span></li>
    <li style="--c:#4FC3F7"><strong>Piloting</strong><span>Bring a falling ship down on the pad in a crosswind.</span></li>
    <li style="--c:#9C8CFF"><strong>Stealth</strong><span>Getting past people who are paid to see you.</span></li>
    <li style="--c:#3DDC97"><strong>Hacking</strong><span>Anything with a circuit and an opinion.</span></li>
    <li style="--c:#5EE6E6"><strong>Repair</strong><span>Keeping a ship that should be scrap in one piece.</span></li>
    <li style="--c:#FFB547"><strong>Crafting</strong><span>Heat it, shape it, sell it.</span></li>
    <li style="--c:#FF8AC4"><strong>Social</strong><span>Talking people into it, out of it, or down on price.</span></li>
    <li style="--c:#C8E06B"><strong>Navigation</strong><span>Plotting a lane that gets you there with fuel to spare.</span></li>
  </ul>
