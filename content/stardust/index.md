<section class="sd-section" id="garage" aria-labelledby="sd-garage-title">
  <div class="sd-section-head"><h2 id="sd-garage-title">Build the ship you want to fly</h2><p class="sd-meta">Needle · Manta · Wisp · Original Courier</p></div>
  <p class="sd-copy">Choose a family, swap body, wings, cockpit and engines, then make it yours with paint, palettes and layered decals. Compare the build's speed, thrust, grip, boost and brake in the garage before you equip it.</p>
  <p class="sd-copy"><strong>Your build changes the flight.</strong> Equipped garage ships use their own stats and hitbox in the five circuits, full-network runs and ranked weekly attempts. They share the same leaderboards. Fly without a custom build equipped to use the standard ship.</p>
  <p class="sd-copy">Save and export editable designs, browse other pilots' work, or sign in to publish your own and leave comments. Text decals use preset sayings. Your equipped ship, flight settings and design library can sync with your account; the in-game sync status tells you when they're saved.</p>

<section class="sd-section" id="how-it-plays" aria-labelledby="sd-how-title">
  <div class="sd-section-head"><h2 id="sd-how-title">How it plays</h2></div>
  <p class="sd-copy">The five network circuits start and end at a portal. Launch, fly the checkpoints in order, pick up the apex shards along the line, and bring the ship back through the portal to finish the lap. Boost gives a short push and recharges on its own. Asteroids break if you shoot them, and hurt if you don't.</p>
  <p class="sd-copy">Make the cockpit fit you in <strong>Flight settings</strong>: switch between track view and a behind-ship camera, tune the view, rebind keys and controller buttons, and move the HUD and touch controls. Copy a settings code to share your setup. The controls below are the defaults; Dogfight keeps its own standard mapping.</p>
  <div class="sd-controls">
    <div>
      <h3>Keyboard</h3>

        <div><dt>Thrust · Reverse</dt><dd>W S or ↑ ↓</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Strafe</dt><dd>Q E</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Launch from the portal</dt><dd>Space</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Boost · Brake · Fire</dt><dd>Shift · X · Ctrl</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Camera · Pause · Map · Full screen</dt><dd>C · Esc · M · F</dd></div>
      </dl>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h3>Gamepad</h3>
      <dl>
        <div><dt>Thrust · Reverse · Strafe</dt><dd>Left stick</dd></div>

        <div><dt>Launch</dt><dd>A</dd></div>

  <div class="sd-section-head"><h2 id="sd-dogfight-title">Dogfight with friends</h2><p class="sd-meta">Private rooms · 1v1 or three-player free-for-all</p></div>
  <p class="sd-copy">Create a room and share its eight-letter code for a duel, or bring a third pilot for a casual free-for-all. Pick a Light, Medium or Heavy Dogfight ship, then thrust, brake and fire until one ship is left. In a 1v1 with both pilots signed in, wins count on your account. Three-player matches are casual, with no saved rankings or match history.</p>
