<section class="sd-section" id="how-it-plays" aria-labelledby="sd-how-title">
  <div class="sd-section-head"><h2 id="sd-how-title">How it plays</h2></div>
  <p class="sd-copy">Every circuit starts and ends at the same portal. Launch, fly the checkpoints in order, pick up the apex shards along the line, and bring the ship back through the portal to finish the lap. Boost gives a short push and recharges on its own. Asteroids break if you shoot them, and hurt if you don't.</p>
  <div class="sd-controls">
    <div>
      <h3>Keyboard</h3>

        <div><dt>Strafe</dt><dd>Q E</dd></div>

        <div><dt>Boost · Brake · Fire</dt><dd>Shift · X · Ctrl</dd></div>

      </dl>
    </div>
    <div>
