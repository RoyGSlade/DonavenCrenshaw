<p class="section-desc">Free software by Donaven Crenshaw.</p>

underplain is the free-software branch: useful tools, source available, and MIT licensed. The software is free of charge. There are no crippled paid editions. Optional paid installation, customization, deployment, training, and support belong to [Crenshaw Systems](../crenshaw-systems/), not to a locked product tier.

## The first three tools

| Product | Status | What is true today | Next proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [BetterFingers](./betterfingers/) | **Private alpha** | Feature-locked and polishing toward public alpha. It is not released. | Clean installation and complete voice-to-draft-to-review demonstration. |
| [GetFast](./getfast/) | **Concept** | The intent is documented; no public implementation, compatibility evidence, or download is published here. | Technical dossier and a real large-download demonstration. |
| [PDFManager](./pdfmanager/) | **Concept** | The intended scope is listed; the implemented subset, build, and compatibility are not proven here. | Product dossier, test documents, and an end-to-end demonstration. |

These statuses use the shared vocabulary: Concept, Prototype, Private alpha, Public alpha, Beta, Stable, Maintenance, and Archived. A page does not turn an idea into a release.

## What “free” means here

underplain software is free of charge, source available, and MIT licensed. Each product still needs its own repository, license file, release evidence, and limitations before it is promoted as ready to download. Missing proof is called out instead of filled with marketing copy.

Support helps pay for polishing, releases, compute, hosting, signing, testing, and project work. Support does not buy ownership, a promised date, or control over the roadmap.

## Current branch boundary

The tools here are public software work. Crenshaw Systems owns and delivers the optional paid services around them, including installation, customization, deployment, training, or support. That service relationship does not turn a free underplain product into a paid edition.
