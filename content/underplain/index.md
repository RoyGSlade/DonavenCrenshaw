<div class="principle-row" aria-label="Underplain principles">
<article class="noir-card noir-card--lit principle-card"><h3>Free of charge</h3></article>

Each product still needs its own repository, license file, release evidence, and limitations before it is promoted as ready to download. Missing proof is called out instead of filled with marketing copy.

Support helps pay for polishing, releases, compute, hosting, signing, testing, and project work. Support does not buy ownership, a promised date, or control over the roadmap.
</section>

<section class="noir-card lit-edge shadow-caster underplain-section">
<h2>Current branch boundary</h2>

The tools here are public software work. Crenshaw Systems owns and delivers the optional paid services around them, including installation, customization, deployment, training, or support. That service relationship does not turn a free underplain product into a paid edition.
