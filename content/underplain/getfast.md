<p class="mono">CONCEPT · INTENT DOCUMENTED · NO PUBLIC BUILD</p>

## Intent

GetFast is intended to combine multiple available network paths to assist large downloads. Steam downloads and browser downloads are the intended targets. The intended behavior is to activate intelligently and avoid unnecessary operation.

## What is not proven

This page describes intent, not an implementation. There is no public GetFast build, compatibility matrix, performance result, source link, or demonstration identified here. The repository does not currently establish bandwidth aggregation, Steam compatibility, browser compatibility, or a guaranteed speed improvement.

## Limitations

- Do not assume that multiple connections can be combined on every network or service.
- Do not assume that Steam or browser downloads are supported until a working build demonstrates them.
- Do not assume that GetFast will improve a particular download.
- Privacy, local operation, failure recovery, and network behavior still require a product dossier.

## Next proof

Before a public alpha, GetFast needs a technical definition, a real implementation, a controlled large-download demonstration, platform and network requirements, failure behavior, source, and an MIT license file.

GetFast is part of underplain's free-of-charge, source-available, MIT-licensed model when it becomes a published product. There is no paid edition to buy and no download link until a real artifact exists.
