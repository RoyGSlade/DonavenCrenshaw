<section class="kc-section kc-first-run" id="first-run" aria-labelledby="first-run-title">
  <div class="kc-section-heading"><p class="kc-eyebrow">YOUR FIRST RUN</p><h2 id="first-run-title">Build the city that will keep you alive.</h2><p>You start with ten civilians and a supply of food and planks. Here is a useful opening, with room to find your own.</p></div>
  <ol class="kc-opening">
    <li><span class="kc-step">01</span><div><h3>Secure food.</h3><p>Place a farm near the granary. Let teamsters finish it, then keep it staffed. Your starting food will not last forever.</p></div></li>
    <li><span class="kc-step">02</span><div><h3>Connect the wood economy.</h3><p>Put a woodcutter near trees and a sawmill along the route to the storehouse. Click buildings to adjust workers and read production status.</p></div></li>
    <li><span class="kc-step">03</span><div><h3>Make room, then recruit.</h3><p>Add housing while keeping spare food. Build a barracks and turn some citizens into defenders. Keep enough civilians to supply the town.</p></div></li>
  </ol>
  <div class="kc-pressure"><strong>Before each invasion, make a choice.</strong><p>Another farm, a house, a road, or another archer? If the city needs workers back, demobilize a selected soldier. Watch the invasion warning, use the minimap to find the approach, and spend a freeze on one deliberate construction when the timing matters.</p></div>
  <div class="kc-chain" aria-label="Wood supply chain"><span>Woodcutter</span><b aria-hidden="true">→</b><span>Logs</span><b aria-hidden="true">→</b><span>Sawmill</span><b aria-hidden="true">→</b><span>Planks in storage</span><b aria-hidden="true">→</b><span>Your next building</span></div>

<section class="kc-section" id="play-guide" aria-labelledby="play-guide-title">
  <div class="kc-section-heading"><p class="kc-eyebrow">GET PLAYING</p><h2 id="play-guide-title">Install. Launch. Build.</h2><p>Windows x64, mouse, and keyboard. No Godot or Blender installation, game account, or connection is needed to play after downloading.</p></div>
  <div class="kc-guide-grid">
    <article class="kc-panel"><h3>Install the playtest</h3><ol><li>Download and extract the small Windows installer ZIP.</li><li>Run <code>KingdomsAndCaravans.exe</code> and choose <strong>Install and play</strong>. It downloads and verifies the full game.</li><li>Begin a new kingdom or load your existing save. The same executable works offline after installation.</li></ol><p class="kc-small">Windows may warn about this unsigned executable. The release page identifies the build and provides its checksum. Keep Windows security protections enabled.</p></article>
    <article class="kc-panel"><h3>Update when you choose</h3><p><strong>Coming from 0.2.1 or 0.3.0?</strong> Use your existing updater. The small compatibility installer downloads and verifies the full game while keeping your save and old installation.<br><br>Select <strong>Check for updates</strong> in the menu. If a newer build is offered, choose <strong>Download update</strong>, then <strong>Restart with update</strong> when ready.</p><p>Your kingdom is saved before switching. If the download fails or is cancelled, your previous shortcut stays usable. After installation the same shortcut opens the game offline. GitHub checks and downloads need an internet connection.</p><p class="kc-small">For installation without a connection on the playing PC, download the <a href="https://github.com/RoyGSlade/KingdomsAndCaravans/releases/download/v0.3.2/KingdomsAndCaravans-full-windows.zip">full Windows ZIP</a> on another PC and extract it. Keep your old folder for rollback.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="kc-controls"><h3>The controls that matter</h3><dl><div><dt><kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd></dt><dd>Pan the camera</dd></div><div><dt><kbd>Q</kbd><kbd>E</kbd> / <kbd>R</kbd></dt><dd>Rotate / reset camera</dd></div><div><dt>Mouse wheel</dt><dd>Zoom in and out</dd></div><div><dt><kbd>Tab</kbd> / <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>0</kbd></dt><dd>Cycle categories / select visible building</dd></div><div><dt>Hover or click a building</dt><dd>Read description and costs</dd></div><div><dt><kbd>Space</kbd></dt><dd>Freeze / play; one construction per freeze</dd></div><div><dt>Drag a selection box</dt><dd>Select your troops</dd></div><div><dt>Right click / <kbd>H</kbd></dt><dd>Move, post, attack, or hold</dd></div><div><dt><kbd>Esc</kbd></dt><dd>Cancel placement</dd></div><div><dt><kbd>F5</kbd> / <kbd>F9</kbd></dt><dd>Save / load confirmation</dd></div></dl></div>
  <div class="kc-faq"><h3>A few practical answers</h3>
    <details><summary>Where is my save? Can I keep my current kingdom?</summary><div><p>Saves stay on this computer in the Godot app-data folder for Kingdoms &amp; Caravans. The current build uses <code>kingdom-city-v2.json</code> and keeps a <code>.bak</code> backup. Use Save kingdom and Load kingdom in the menu. Legacy 0.2.x saves retain their original map dimensions; start a new kingdom to see the full current map and guided opening.</p><p>Keep a backup before testing an early preview.</p></div></details>
    <details><summary>Will it run on my PC?</summary><div><p>The current download is for Windows x64. It has been checked on the development laptop, but minimum hardware requirements have not been established. Mac, Linux, mobile, controller, and browser builds are not provided in this playtest.</p><p>If it fails to start or runs poorly, include your Windows version and graphics hardware in a bug report.</p></div></details>
    <details><summary>Does the game need the internet?</summary><div><p>Gameplay and local saves work offline. The initial installer and manually requested updates contact GitHub. There are no game accounts or analytics.</p></div></details>
    <details><summary>What happens after the second invasion?</summary><div><p>If your castle survives both invasions, you win and can continue building the same city without another invasion. Start another kingdom to try a different opening. This build does not include a campaign, caravans, or multiplayer.</p></div></details>
