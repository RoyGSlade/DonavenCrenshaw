<section class="kc-section kc-first-run" id="first-run" aria-labelledby="first-run-title">
  <div class="kc-section-heading"><p class="kc-eyebrow">YOUR FIRST RUN</p><h2 id="first-run-title">Build the city that will keep you alive.</h2><p>You start with ten civilians and a supply of food and planks. Here is a useful opening, with room to find your own.</p></div>
  <ol class="kc-opening">
    <li><span class="kc-step">01</span><div><h3>Secure food.</h3><p>Place a farm near the granary. Let teamsters finish it, then keep it staffed. Your starting food will not last forever.</p></div></li>
    <li><span class="kc-step">02</span><div><h3>Connect the wood economy.</h3><p>Put a woodcutter near trees and a sawmill along the route to the storehouse. Click buildings to adjust workers and read production status.</p></div></li>
    <li><span class="kc-step">03</span><div><h3>Make room, then recruit.</h3><p>Add housing while keeping spare food. Build a barracks and turn some citizens into defenders. Keep enough civilians to supply the town.</p></div></li>
  </ol>
  <div class="kc-pressure"><strong>Before each invasion, make a choice.</strong><p>Another farm, a house, or another archer? If the city needs workers back, demobilize a selected soldier. Watch the invasion warning and use the minimap to find the approach.</p></div>

<section class="kc-section" id="play-guide" aria-labelledby="play-guide-title">
  <div class="kc-section-heading"><p class="kc-eyebrow">GET PLAYING</p><h2 id="play-guide-title">Extract. Launch. Build.</h2><p>Windows x64, mouse, and keyboard. No Godot or Blender installation, game account, or connection is needed to play after downloading.</p></div>
  <div class="kc-guide-grid">
    <article class="kc-panel"><h3>Install the playtest</h3><ol><li>Download the 0.2.1 ZIP.</li><li>Extract the entire ZIP into a folder.</li><li>Run <code>KingdomsAndCaravans.exe</code> and choose <strong>Begin your kingdom</strong>.</li></ol><p class="kc-small">Windows may warn about this unsigned executable. The release page identifies the build and provides its checksum. Keep Windows security protections enabled.</p></article>
    <article class="kc-panel"><h3>Update when you choose</h3><p>In the menu, select <strong>Check for updates</strong>. If a newer build is offered, choose <strong>Download update</strong>, then <strong>Restart with update</strong> when ready.</p><p>The verified download installs beside the old version. Your kingdom is saved before switching. GitHub checks and downloads need an internet connection.</p><p class="kc-small">Coming from 0.2.0? Download 0.2.1 once to get the update button.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="kc-controls"><h3>The controls that matter</h3><dl><div><dt><kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd></dt><dd>Pan the camera</dd></div><div><dt><kbd>Q</kbd><kbd>E</kbd> / <kbd>R</kbd></dt><dd>Rotate / reset camera</dd></div><div><dt>Mouse wheel</dt><dd>Zoom in and out</dd></div><div><dt>Click a building</dt><dd>Inspect &amp; assign workers</dd></div><div><dt>Drag a selection box</dt><dd>Select your troops</dd></div><div><dt>Right click / <kbd>H</kbd></dt><dd>Move or attack / hold</dd></div><div><dt><kbd>Esc</kbd></dt><dd>Pause or cancel placement</dd></div><div><dt><kbd>F5</kbd> / <kbd>F9</kbd></dt><dd>Save / load confirmation</dd></div></dl></div>
  <div class="kc-faq"><h3>A few practical answers</h3>
    <details><summary>Where is my save? Can I keep my current kingdom?</summary><div><p>Saves stay on this computer in the Godot app-data folder for Kingdoms &amp; Caravans. Version 0.2 uses <code>kingdom-city-v2.json</code> and keeps a <code>.bak</code> backup. Version 0.2.1 uses the same save format. Use Save kingdom and Load kingdom in the menu.</p><p>Version 0.1 saves require the old build; they are not migrated into 0.2.</p></div></details>
    <details><summary>Will it run on my PC?</summary><div><p>The current download is for Windows x64. It has been checked on the development laptop, but minimum hardware requirements have not been established. Mac, Linux, mobile, controller, and browser builds are not provided in this playtest.</p><p>If it fails to start or runs poorly, include your Windows version and graphics hardware in a bug report.</p></div></details>
    <details><summary>Does the game need the internet?</summary><div><p>Gameplay and local saves work offline. Only manually checking and downloading updates contacts GitHub. There are no game accounts or analytics.</p></div></details>
    <details><summary>What happens after the second invasion?</summary><div><p>If your castle survives both invasions, you win and the current run ends. Start another kingdom to try a different opening. This build does not include a campaign, caravans, or progression between runs.</p></div></details>
